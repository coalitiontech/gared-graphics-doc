Site settings¶

Your logo, contact details, header, footer and social links are set once and used across the whole site. You change them in the Customizer, which shows a live preview of the site as you type.

Opening the Customizer¶

Go to Appearance → Customize in the left menu (or click Customize in the dark bar at the top of the site while you're logged in).

  1. Header: logo, phone number and the button at the top right.
  2. Contact details: phone, email, address and opening hours.
  3. Page header: the default photo at the top of pages.
  4. Call to action: the burgundy band at the end of blog posts and projects.
  5. Footer: footer logo, headings, copyright line and social links.
  6. Menus: the links in the header and footer.
  7. Publish: nothing changes on the live site until you click it.

Preview first, then publish

Every change shows straight away in the preview on the right, but visitors don't see it yet. When you're happy, click Publish at the top. If you change your mind, close the Customizer with the ✕ at the top left and leave without publishing.

Header¶

  1. Logo: click Change image to pick a new logo from the Media Library or upload one. Use a PNG with a transparent background, at least 440 pixels wide, so it stays sharp on high-resolution screens. The same logo is used in the footer and in the emails the forms send, unless you set a separate footer logo.
  2. Logo width on desktop: how wide the logo appears on large screens, between 120 and 220 pixels (180 is the design's size). On phones and tablets it's shown smaller automatically.
  3. Show phone number: shows or hides the phone number next to the menu.
  4. Header button label: the text on the burgundy button at the top right (currently Request a Quote).
  5. Header button URL: where the button goes. /contact-us/#quote opens the quote form on the Contact page; leave it empty to link to the Contact Us page.

Contact details¶

These are used everywhere they appear on the site: the header, the footer, the Contact page and the contact sections on other pages. Change them here once and they update everywhere.

  1. Phone (as displayed): the number exactly as visitors should see it, e.g. (805) 987-8577. It's also made into a tap-to-call link.
  2. Phone link override: leave empty. Only needed if the number people should dial differs from the one shown (for example with an extension), written as tel:+18059878577.
  3. Email: the email address shown on the site. This doesn't change where form messages are delivered.
  4. Facility address: one line per row. It's shown on the Contact page and used for the map and the Get directions link.
  5. Business hours: one line per row, shown on the Contact page.

Page header¶

Default image: the photo shown at the top of pages that don't have their own Featured Image. To give a single page its own photo, set the Featured Image when editing that page. Leave the default empty for a plain dark band.

Call to action¶

The burgundy band shown at the end of blog posts, portfolio projects and the "page not found" page:

  • Heading: the band's headline.
  • Button label and Button URL: the button's text and link (leave the URL empty to link to the Contact Us page).

Footer¶

  1. Footer logo: leave empty to use the header logo, or choose a different version for the dark footer.
  2. Footer logo width: between 100 and 240 pixels (160 is the design's size).
  3. Menu heading: the title above the footer menu (currently Explore).
  4. Contact heading: the title above the footer's contact details (currently Contact).
  5. Show phone number and Show email: show or hide them in the footer.
  6. Copyright line: leave empty for "© {year} Gared Graphics. All rights reserved."; the year updates itself.

Social links¶

Scroll down in the Footer section for the social media addresses.

  1. Facebook URL
  2. Instagram URL
  3. X (Twitter) URL
  4. Pinterest URL
  5. LinkedIn URL

Paste the full address of your profile page, e.g. https://www.facebook.com/yourpage. Each one shows as an icon in the contact panel on the Contact page; leave a field empty to hide that icon.

Menus¶

Appearance → Menus (or Menus in the Customizer) controls three menus:

Menu Where it appears
Primary The main menu in the header
Footer The links in the footer
Legal The small links next to the copyright line (Sitemap, Terms of Use)

To add a page, tick it in the list on the left, click Add to Menu, drag it into place, then click Save Menu. Drag items to change their order; click an item's arrow and Remove to take it off the menu.

Site Identity¶

Site Identity in the Customizer holds the Site Title (used in browser tabs, search results and the copyright line), the Tagline, and the Site Icon: the small image shown in browser tabs. Use a square image at least 512 × 512 pixels.
